## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Supplier Risk & Performance Analytics** for the **Manufacturing & Supply Chain** domain.

The objective is to analyze supplier performance, identify high-risk suppliers, build a supplier watchlist, and support procurement teams with data-driven decisions.

The dashboard uses supplier financial, delivery, quality, compliance, sustainability, ERP transaction, and incident data to monitor supplier risk and performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

<span style="color:red"><b>Supplier failures can disrupt production, delay deliveries, increase cost, and damage customer satisfaction.</b></span>

Manufacturing companies need an early-warning analytics system to identify risky suppliers before they create supply chain disruptions.

---

## ✅ Business Solution

This project builds a complete analytics solution using:

* 📊 **Power BI Dashboard** for supplier risk monitoring
* 🐍 **Python EDA** for data analysis and insights
* 🤖 **Machine Learning Model** for risk prediction
* 📁 **Supplier Watchlist** for procurement action
* 📈 **Feature Importance Analysis** to understand key risk drivers

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

<span style="color:red"><b>Insight 1:</b></span> Almost half of supplier records are classified as **High Risk**, showing strong exposure to supply chain disruption.

<span style="color:orange"><b>Insight 2:</b></span> **2025 has the highest high-risk percentage**, so recent supplier performance needs urgent review.

<span style="color:blue"><b>Insight 3:</b></span> **Financial Stability** and **Delivery Performance** are the strongest drivers of supplier risk.

<span style="color:green"><b>Insight 4:</b></span> Supplier incidents should be tracked separately because incident volume may reveal operational risk not fully captured by score-based risk labels.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

* 🚨 Create a **Supplier Risk Watchlist** for high-risk suppliers
* 📦 Prepare backup suppliers for critical materials
* 📉 Reduce dependency on suppliers with repeated incidents
* 🧾 Perform financial and compliance audits for risky suppliers
* 📊 Monitor supplier performance quarterly
* 🤖 Use AI prediction scores for proactive procurement decisions
